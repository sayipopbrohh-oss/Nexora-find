Support & Policies

We dispatch every order within 24–48 hours of confirmation. Standard delivery takes 3–6 business days depending on your location; metro cities typically see 2–4 days. Free shipping applies on orders over ₹999 — a flat ₹79 fee applies below that. You'll get SMS and email tracking updates the moment your order ships.

Changed your mind? You can request a return within 7 days of delivery, as long as the item is unused, undamaged, and in its original packaging. Once we receive and inspect the return, refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. Cash on Delivery refunds are processed via bank transfer or UPI.

We collect only the information needed to process and deliver your order — name, address, phone, and payment details — and never sell your data to third parties. Information is stored securely and used solely for order fulfilment, customer support, and (if you opt in) product updates via our newsletter.

By placing an order on Nexora.Find, you agree to provide accurate delivery and payment information. Prices are listed in Indian Rupees (₹) and may change without prior notice. Product images are representative; minor variations in colour or packaging may occur. Nexora.Find reserves the right to cancel orders in cases of suspected fraud or stock unavailability.

Email us at support@nexorafind.in or reach out on Instagram/X @nexorafind — our team typically replies within a few hours, every day of the week.
